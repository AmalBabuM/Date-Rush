DATE RUSH

Launch "Date Rush.exe" and choose LET'S HARVEST.
Keep the executable, Date Rush_Data folder and adjacent DLL files together.

A / D or arrows: move
Space / W / up: jump
Hold Shift with a movement key to sprint.
P / Escape: pause / resume
Mouse / touch: drag along the floor to move
Top-left pause button: pause

Catch the announced variety. Check the current target in the pause menu.
Collect 15 matching dates in
90 seconds. Correct catches score 100-200 points; wrong dates cost 25.
Jump over the snake's head to avoid a 50-point penalty.
Open About Dates from the pause menu to see all six dates and their facts.

The collection explains six date varieties. Your discoveries and best
score are saved locally.
